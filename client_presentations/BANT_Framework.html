BANT Assessment Framework

Qualifying and structuring GCP migration opportunities

The BANT framework (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) provides a structured approach to qualify and assess GCP migration opportunities. By systematically evaluating these four key areas, Sales Engineers can effectively prioritize efforts, create targeted solutions, and establish realistic implementation plans that align with customer constraints and objectives.

GCP Opportunity Qualification
Budget
Financial capacity and investment readiness
Authority
Decision-making power and stakeholder alignment
Need
Pain points and business drivers
Timeline
Implementation urgency and project scheduling

BANT Assessment Checklist

Budget

What is the customer's allocated budget for cloud migration?

Identify specific budget range and funding source

Has a preliminary TCO analysis been conducted?

Compare current costs vs. projected GCP costs

What is the customer's purchasing process?

Understand procurement cycle and approval thresholds

What is the expected ROI timeframe?

Determine acceptable payback period for investment

Authority

Who are the key decision makers for this initiative?

Map the decision-making hierarchy and roles

What is the approval process for technology purchases?

Understand governance structure and controls

Are there potential internal blockers or competing priorities?

Identify potential resistance points or conflicting initiatives

Who are the technical influencers on the customer team?

Identify key technical stakeholders who influence decisions

Need

What business problems is the customer trying to solve?

Connect technical needs to business outcomes

What are their current pain points with existing infrastructure?

Identify operational, cost, and performance challenges

How does GCP specifically address their technical requirements?

Match GCP capabilities to customer's technical needs

What is the potential business impact if needs aren't addressed?

Quantify cost of inaction or status quo

Timeline

What is the desired implementation timeline?

Establish realistic expectations for migration phases

Are there any fixed deadlines driving the project?

Identify time-sensitive business events or constraints

What is the customer's readiness for implementation?

Assess team capabilities and resource availability

What is the customer's preferred phasing approach?

Determine workload prioritization and migration sequence

  • Focus on high-ROI components
  • Develop phased implementation
  • Identify cost optimization

Nurture

Financial potential but unclear technical fit

  • Conduct needs assessment
  • Provide education on GCP capabilities
  • Build detailed technical mapping

Qualify

Early stage opportunities requiring development

  • Focus on business value
  • Build ROI case for investment
  • Reassess regularly for changes
